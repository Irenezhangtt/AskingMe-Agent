## Why balanced parentheses were not enough

`Subtract CNY 50 (except refurbished items)` has balanced punctuation even if a splitter puts the discount and its exception in separate chunks. The scope guard now keeps the entire logical paragraph together. An attached `Except …` note in the next paragraph also remains connected to its antecedent.

The planner is explicitly instructed to distinguish these forms, and uploaded-policy calculations now require cited `rule_checks` explaining their scope in the final answer:

- **Arithmetic grouping:** `(original - threshold - coupon) × member multiplier`.
- **Eligibility restrictions:** `(only if the original subtotal reaches CNY 300)`.
- **Exceptions and nested overrides:** `(except refurbished items (unless Gold status applies))`.
- **Boolean scope:** `A AND (B OR C)` versus `(A AND B) OR C`.
- **Illustrations:** `(example: a CNY 500 order saves CNY 50)` does not create an additional CNY 50 coupon.
- **Caps, floors and one-time limits:** a cap on the discount is different from a cap on the payable amount.

Malformed uploaded parentheses trigger clarification before the pricing model is called. Integrity checks protect structure; they do not prove the semantic correctness of model interpretation. The golden set separately tests these semantics.

## Correct metric definitions

- **Recall@5:** relevant document IDs found in the first five returned chunks, deduplicated for the numerator, divided by the labeled relevant-document count. Duplicate chunks do not inflate recall.
- **Faithfulness:** supported generated factual claims divided by generated factual claims. No factual claims means missing data, not a fabricated perfect score.
- **Reference-claim recall:** covered reference claims divided by reference claims. For example, two of five reference claims covered is 40% recall, not 40% faithfulness.
- **Exact-price rate:** computed currency/amount matches the independent numeric label. It cannot establish retrieval quality, OCR accuracy or semantic faithfulness by itself.
