# Demo Promotion Boundaries and Refunds

These fictional rules apply only to the Appliances category during the Demo Promotion. All amounts are in CNY.

## Threshold boundaries

An original subtotal of exactly CNY 300 qualifies for the CNY 50 threshold discount. CNY 299.99 does not. An original subtotal of exactly CNY 200 satisfies the amount requirement for the CNY 20 appliance coupon, but category, claim status, and validity requirements still apply.

## Missing information

If the original price, product category, membership status, coupon claim status, or promotion name is missing, ask for that information first. Do not infer eligibility from a previous worked example.

## Refunds

These example documents do not define discount allocation for refunds, combined orders across stores, or shipping charges. Refer these questions to the promotion rule owner. Do not substitute rules from another promotion.
